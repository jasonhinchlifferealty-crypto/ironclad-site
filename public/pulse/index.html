Latest · Edition 1 · November 18, 2026

Sales are absorbing the shock. Prices have stopped rising.

New Brunswick home sales are tracking 7.5% below 2025. Forecasts published a year ago called for a gain of 4% to 7%. The benchmark price was $339,400 in October, 3.6% below May. We expect flat values over the next 12 months, not a reversal.
